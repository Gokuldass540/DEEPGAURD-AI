College Mini-Project Project Overview

About DeepGuard AI

DeepGuard AI is an AI-based media verification platform engineered to help users identify potentially manipulated or synthetic images and videos.

The Problem

Advances in generative artificial intelligence (GANs, Diffusion models, FaceSwap) make synthetic deepfakes virtually indistinguishable to the human eye. Misinformation, identity impersonation, and fraudulent media pose severe threats to digital trust.

The Solution

DeepGuard AI provides an accessible, automated media analysis platform. By combining OpenCV facial detection with multi-spectral FFT frequency analysis and PyTorch neural feature classification, DeepGuard flags manipulation indicators with calibrated confidence metrics.

Technology Stack

AI & Computer Vision
PyTorch, OpenCV, NumPy, Pillow
Frontend UI
